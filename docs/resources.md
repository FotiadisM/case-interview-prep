# Recommended Resources

A ranked, opinionated guide to case-interview resources, built from 2026 reviews and platform comparisons. The honest hierarchy: **free firm materials → peer practice → structured courses/drills → coaching for final calibration.** Nothing is required beyond the free tier — most offers are won with free materials + discipline — but most candidates benefit from 1–2 paid tools matched to their bottleneck.

## Tier 0 — Official firm materials (free, non-negotiable)

Use these before anything else; they show the exact expected answer style.

- [McKinsey practice cases & Interview Prep](https://www.mckinsey.com/careers/interviewing) — 8 official cases with coaching videos; the model answers *are* the standard.
- [BCG case interview preparation](https://careers.bcg.com/global/en/case-interview-preparation) — interactive case, written-case example PDF.
- [Bain case interview prep](https://www.bain.com/careers/hiring-process/case-interview) — practice cases + advice from Bainies.

## Tier 1 — Practice platforms & course libraries (paid, high ROI)

| Platform | Cost (approx.) | Best for | Verdict from reviews |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PrepLounge** | ~€79/yr Premium | Unlimited peer mocks, 575k+ member community, consulting forum | The highest-ROI single purchase *if you actually use the peer mocks* (Road to Offer). Marketplace coach quality varies — read reviews before booking. |
| **CaseCoach** | ~€172/yr | Structured video curriculum by named ex-MBB instructors; case/fit/PEI math courses; practice room | Strong sequence for beginners→intermediate; some users find the structuring drills mixed; McKinsey-heavy per some reviews. |
| **CraftingCases** | course-based ($) | Deep structuring/framework theory, workbook-style | Best-in-class for *structuring* per PrepLounge coaches; more theory than practice reps — pair with live mocks. |

| **Management Consulted** | case library + coaching | Huge case/framework library, firm directory, free guides | One of the oldest brands; deep library; quality varies outside core guides. |
| **RocketBlocks** | ~$155/yr | Isolated drills: math, structuring, charts, brainstorming, with progress tracking | Excellent supplement, not a standalone prep (builds skills before full cases). |
| **CaseBasix** | freemium | Assessment sims (Solve, Casey, SOVA), industry primers, drills | Noteworthy free tier for online-test simulation; framed for the 2026 assessment stack. |
| **MyConsultingCoach** | course/subscription | Problem-driven approach methodology, Solve/Casey guides | Respected methodology alternative to memorized frameworks. |

### AI practice tools (2026 category)

AI interviewers give instant scored feedback when partners are scarce: Road to Offer (57+ scored voice cases, Casey sim, drills), Merra, CaseCasestudy tools, Soreno, and others appear in 2026 comparisons. Caveat identical across reviews: AI feedback quality varies by category (structure and math score well; business judgment scores weakest). Use AI for reps and volume; use humans (peers, then coaches) for calibration — and never during the real interview (McKinsey explicitly rejects AI use live).

### Coaching (final calibration)

Marketplaces (PrepLounge, IGotAnOffer, Leland) list ex-MBB interviewers at $150–300/hr with widely varying quality — shortlist by verified reviews and MBB interviewer history. One or two sessions in the final 2–3 weeks (ideally with someone from your target firm) fixes what self-review can't see.

## Tier 2 — Free curated guidance & libraries

These are the sites this playbook draws from; all have deep free sections worth reading:

- [CaseXcel – Consulting frameworks](https://www.casexcel.com/consulting-frameworks) — concise, current framework guidance.

- [IGotAnOffer](https://igotanoffer.com/) — blog + broad coaching marketplace.

## MBA casebooks (free, high volume)

University consulting clubs publish full casebooks with interviewer notes and model answers — the classic free practice corpus. This site ships four (Kellogg 2020, MIT Sloan 2020, Tuck 2024, Yale 2013) in [case libraries](/case-libraries). Others — Columbia, Wharton, Duke Fuqua, LBS, INSEAD, Darden — circulate on PrepLounge, HackingTheCaseInterview's free-resources page, and clubs' own sites.

## Books & video

- **Books:** *Case in Point* (Cosentino) — breadth of frameworks, uses older fixed-framework style (memorizing them is now penalized; use as a reference); *Case Interview Secrets* (Victor Cheng) — mindset and thinking-out-loud; *The Pyramid Principle* (Minto) — the communication standard behind every synthesis score.
- **YouTube:** CraftingCases walkthroughs, firm career channels, Management Consulted case walks, PrepLounge expert-case video solutions — watch to internalize signposting and tone.

## How to assemble your stack (recommended combos)

- **Budget $0, 6+ weeks:** firm materials + this playbook's [casebooks](/case-libraries) + PrepLounge free tier + YouTube — fully sufficient for many offers.
- **Budget ~$150, 4–8 weeks:** PrepLounge Premium (peer mocks) + RocketBlocks (drills) — the drills-then-reps loop.
- **Budget ~$400+:** add CaseCoach or HackingTheCaseInterview course for methodology, and 1–2 coaching sessions in the final weeks.
- **Short timeline (≤2 weeks):** AI-scored case reps daily (Road to Offer/RocketBlocks/Merra free tiers), drills every morning, one coach session immediately, and your target firm's official videos.
