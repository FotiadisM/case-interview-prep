# Marketing Cases (Extended)

Marketing cases cover brand positioning, go-to-market, demand generation, and commercial effectiveness. The cleanest recognizable version: "our market share is falling," which can be a marketing case in disguise. The disciplined sequence is **5Cs → STP → 4Ps**: diagnose, then target, then execute — each stage derived from the previous one.

Source: [Road to Offer – Marketing case](https://www.roadtooffer.com/blog/marketing-case-interview), [HackingTheCaseInterview – Marketing case](https://www.hackingthecaseinterview.com/pages/marketing-case-interview), [PrepLounge – 4P framework](https://www.preplounge.com/en/case-interview-basics/4p)

## The 5C–STP–4P framework

**Stage 1 — 5Cs (situational diagnosis):**
- **Company** — brand, capabilities, economics, where we win.
- **Customers** — segments, needs, buying process, willingness to pay, satisfaction.
- **Competitors** — positioning, share, spend, offerings.
- **Collaborators** — channels, partners, retailers, agencies.
- **Context** — trends, seasonality, regulation, substitutes.

**Stage 2 — STP (strategic targeting):**
- **Segmentation** — how the market genuinely splits (needs/behavior > demographics).
- **Targeting** — pick on segment attractiveness (size, growth, penetration) × ability to win (brand fit, cost to serve). Not always the largest — the best intersection.
- **Positioning** — "for [target], [brand] is the [frame] that [benefit] because [reason to believe]."

**Stage 3 — 4Ps (execution):**
- **Product** — offering, features, packaging, brand.
- **Price** — from [pricing logic](/core/pricing), not decoration.
- **Place** — distribution channels matching where the target shops.
- **Promotion** — message, media mix, spend level; awareness → conversion funnel.

**Close with metrics:** CAC, LTV, share by segment, brand awareness, conversion, NPS — and a quantified revenue/share impact per recommendation.

## When it's really a profitability or growth case

Many "marketing" declines are revenue problems inside a profitability case. Rule: confirm the cause is commercial (share loss, pricing power, awareness, funnel) before going full 5C-STP-4P; otherwise the profitability tree should lead.

## Key factors and considerations

- **The chain must be explicit** — because diagnosis shows X (5Cs), the target is Y (STP), therefore execution is Z (4Ps). A missing link = grounded tactics with no strategy.
- **Funnel math** — awareness → consideration → trial → repeat: find the leaky stage before spending on the top.
- **ROAS and attribution** — every marketing dollar must be justified; cases increasingly test channel-economics instinct (LTV/CAC, marginal ROAS).
- **Positioning vs promotion** — positioning is the strategy; promotion is its loudspeaker. Confusing the two is a common tell.
- **Mental availability vs loyalty** — for most consumer categories, penetration growth beats loyalty deepening (Byron Sharp thinking) — a sophisticated point to raise in FMCG cases.

## Common pitfalls

1. **Skipping the diagnosis** — 4Ps tactics laid over an unverified problem.
2. **Treating the largest segment as the target** — the ability-to-win criterion matters as much.
3. **Marketing without numbers** — budgets, CPMs, conversion rates, and LTVs are expected.
4. **Positioning costs money** — claiming "we'll reposition as premium" without willingness-to-pay evidence for the segment.

## Practice prompts

- A 100-year-old cereal brand is losing share to private label. What would you do?
- Design the positioning and launch campaign for a new EV brand.
- Your client's NPS is high but churn is rising in B2B software. Diagnose.
- A retailer wants to double its loyalty-program membership economics.
- A pharma company's blockbuster goes off-patent. Marketing response?
