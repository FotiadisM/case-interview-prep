# McKinsey & Company

McKinsey's interview is the most standardized of the Big 3: it rigidly tests two things — superior problem-solving (the case) and leadership potential (the PEI). Acceptance is widely estimated near 1% of applicants; the full process runs 5–8 weeks.

Source: [CaseStar – McKinsey guide](https://www.casestar.io/guides/mckinsey), [HackingTheCaseInterview – McKinsey process](https://www.hackingthecaseinterview.com/pages/mckinsey-interview-process-and-questions), [CasingLab – McKinsey guide 2026](https://casinglab.com/blog/mckinsey-interview-guide)

## The process

| Stage | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Application | Resume screening — academic bar, leadership, quantified impact |
| 2. McKinsey Solve | Gamified digital assessment (~60–80 min), before interviews |
| 3. First round | 2 interviews, each = case + PEI (~45–60 min) |
| 4. Final round | 2–3 interviews with partners; deeper PEI, harder cases, client-readiness pressure |
| 5. Offer | ~20–40% of first-round candidates reach finals; under 1% of applicants overall get offers |

2026 notes (HackingTheCaseInterview): the US undergraduate recruiting calendar has moved dramatically earlier; McKinsey published an explicit AI policy — using AI to *practice* is encouraged, using it during interviews or assessments is grounds for rejection.

## The interviewer-led case

The interviewer controls the flow: after your opening structure, they tell you which area to explore and assign individual questions — math problems, chart interpretation, brainstorming — one at a time. Each answer is evaluated independently. This means McKinsey prep needs extra reps on:

- **Standalone quant questions** — verbal arithmetic under pressure
- **Exhibit interpretation** — describe → interpret → implication
- **Structured mini-answers** — every response is a tiny answer-first synthesis
- **Keeping your overall tree in mind** — the questions are fragments of it; connect them back ("This supports my revenue-side hypothesis")

Case-type lean: profitability (most common), market entry, growth, operations (McKinsey's Operations practice), and more public-sector cases than BCG/Bain (largest government practice). Market sizing usually appears *inside* larger cases as a question, not standalone.

Official prep: McKinsey publishes practice cases on its careers site, and its digital-assessment page links Solve materials. Watch the firm's own coaching videos — they model exactly the answer style interviewers expect.

## The PEI (Personal Experience Interview)

Runs ~20 minutes inside every interview and counts for roughly **50% of your score** (per former interviewers cited by HackingTheCaseInterview). The 2026 dimensions:

1. **Connection** (Personal Impact) — building trust and trust-based relationships; influencing without authority; resolving conflict.
2. **Drive** (Entrepreneurial Drive) — creating impact through initiative, ownership, resilience in the face of ambiguity and obstacles.
3. **Leadership** (Inclusive Leadership) — leading teams, developing others, creating inclusive environments.
4. **Growth** — learning from failure/feedback and changing approach; self-awareness and iteration.

**The delivery format matters as much as the stories.** The interviewer names a dimension, invites you to tell a story, then drills: what exactly did you do, what were you thinking, how did you respond to pushback. Stories must be deep, first-person, and survive 5+ follow-up questions. Prepare 2 strong stories per dimension; use the STAR structure but let the details stand for the dimension rather than describing feelings. Practice delivering them to a partner who interrogates.

**The structure interviewers reward:** Situation (2 min max) → what *you* did in sequence → quantifiable outcome → a crisp reflection if asked. If a story collapses under drilling, that dimension fails — thin or borrowed stories fall apart by the third follow-up.

## The Solve game

Two current modules in most sittings (Ecosystem Building has been phased out in recent formats — see [Merra's 2026 guide](https://www.trymerra.ai/blog/how-to-prepare-for-an-mbb-case-interview-in-2026-mckinsey-bcg-and-bain-consulting-interview-guide); older guides listing it as core are outdated):

- **Redrock Study (~35 min)** — an interactive research-study case: read exhibits, do percentage and ratio calculations, draw conclusions under time pressure; a Study phase plus six shorter Cases.
- **Sea Wolf (~30 min)** — sequential resource decisions in an ocean-cleanup scenario; adaptive optimization under constraints.

McKinsey scores both *product* (outcome) and *process* (how you got there); think aloud is impossible — your click path is the evidence. Preparation: timing and format familiarity beat raw skill practice, since the game is designed to need no preparation. Pass rates are commonly estimated at ~20–30% by prep communities (unofficial).

## Culture, focus areas & what "fit" means at McKinsey

- **What the firm emphasizes:** leadership and personal impact ("non-hierarchical meritocracy"), values-driven selection, the generalist model with deep practice areas (Corporate Finance, Digital, Operations, Organization, Public Sector, Sustainability).
- **Fit signals that help:** initiative in ambiguity, structured communication even in conversation, comfort with being challenged, an ownership mindset, and knowing *why McKinsey* (network, scale, capability depth) with specifics.
- **What to avoid:** vague "I want to make an impact" answers; claiming stories you can't defend; networking-metrics as your only "why."

## Firm-specific prep checklist (McKinsey)

1. Do the [official practice cases](https://www.mckinsey.com/careers/interviewing) and watch their videos — internalize the answer style.
2. Drill interviewer-led reps: have a partner fire standalone math/exhibit/brainstorm questions.
3. Run Solve format simulators (see [Resources](/resources)) twice before test day; prioritize Redrock-style timed exhibit work.
4. Build and interrogate-test your PEI story bank (2 per dimension).
5. End every case answer with a recommendation — McKinsey interviewers score synthesis separately.
