# Prep Roadmap: 6 Weeks to Interview-Ready

Based on the training plans recommended by former interviewers at Bain and McKinsey (HackingTheCaseInterview, Road to Offer, CaseCoach), and assuming you already know the basics of case interviews. Adapt freely — 3 weeks requires compression; 10+ weeks allows more casebook volume.

## The core loop (every week)

Each week has three fixed habits plus a shifting focus:

1. **2–4 full practice cases** (live with a partner is best; solo/aloud if unavailable)
2. **2–3 drill sessions** — math, structuring, or exhibit reading, 15–20 min each
3. **1 review session** — write down feedback from the week's cases and the one skill that will be next week's focus

Two rules from experienced interviewers:

- **Quality beats quantity.** 5 cases with thorough feedback improve you more than 20 rushed-through without reflection (HackingTheCaseInterview, [case interview types guide](https://www.hackingthecaseinterview.com/pages/case-interview-types)).
- **Always drill the weak behavior, not just more full cases.** If math is the blocker, spend the week on math drills, not four more cases (Road to Offer's tool-comparison guidance).

## Week 1 — Foundations & profitability

- Re-read [Case Interview Basics](/case-interview-basics) and [Frameworks Overview](/frameworks-overview).
- Build your profitability fluency for real: [Core — Profitability](/core/profitability). It is ~30% of first rounds and the building block for everything else.
- Cases: 2 profitability + 1 market sizing (standalone sizing questions).
- Drills: mental math 15 min/day (percentages, breakeven, margin).
- Milestone: you can state a tailored profitability tree in under 2 minutes and isolate a revenue-vs-cost driver with numbers.

## Week 2 — Market entry & market sizing

- [Market entry](/core/market-entry) + [Market sizing](/core/market-sizing) deep dive.
- Cases: 2 market entry (one geographic, one adjacent-product) + 2 market sizing with a partner.
- Drills: top-down and bottom-up sizing, then sanity check against each other.
- Milestone: you reach the "can we win, and how do we enter?" question by minute 7, not minute 20.

## Week 3 — M&A, pricing, new product

- [M&A](/core/mergers-acquisitions) + [Pricing](/core/pricing) + [New product](/core/new-product).
- Cases: 1 M&A (with synergy math), 1 pricing (price-change variant), 1 new product launch.
- Drills: synergy capture math, willingness-to-pay estimation, channel economics.
- Milestone: you never recommend an acquisition before testing build/partner alternatives; you never price before clarifying the objective.

## Week 4 — Growth, ops, and hybrid cases

- [Growth strategy](/core/growth-strategy) + [Operations & cost](/core/operations-cost).
- Cases: 2 hybrids — a growth case that turns into market entry, and a profitability case that turns out to be a cost/operations problem.
- Drills: bottleneck identification on process-flow exhibits; growth lever quantification with real arithmetic.
- Milestone: you can hold your structure while the case changes direction midstream.

## Week 5 — Firm-specific week

- Choose your target firm page ([McKinsey](/firms/mckinsey), [BCG](/firms/bcg), [Bain](/firms/bain)) and:
  - Run cases in that firm's format (interviewer-led for McKinsey, candidate-led for BCG/Bain).
  - Prepare firm fit: PEI stories (McKinsey), conversational fit (BCG), experience stories + collaborative style (Bain).
  - Practice the relevant online assessment (Solve Redrock/Sea Wolf, Casey chatbot, SOVA).
- Cases: 3–4 in target format, including at least one firm-flavored case (e.g., PE diligence at Bain, exhibit-heavy at BCG).
- Drills: exhibit/chart interpretation with "what it says → what it means → so what" format.

## Week 6 — Simulate the real thing

- 4–5 full mock interviews in real interview conditions: timed, video on, no notes other than your sheet, with feedback.
- Take your mocks from your case partner pool, a coach, or an AI interviewer for extra reps (see [Resources](/resources)).
- Review all your written feedback; fix the single most damaging habit first.
- Rest, logistics (quiet room, backup internet), and one light final review the day before.

## Progression checkpoints

| Week | You should be able to... |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Break any prompt into a tailored MECE structure in ~2 min |
| 3 | Do breakeven/margin/share math mentally at conversation speed |
| 4 | Name the type of the case within the first two sentences of the prompt |
| 6 | Deliver an answer-first recommendation with reasons, risks, next steps in 60–90 seconds |

## If you have less time

- 0–2 weeks: prioritize reps. Two cases per day minimum, focus on profitability + market entry, run the target firm's online-assessment simulator (see [Resources](/resources)), and fix math daily. Skip extended case types except those common at your firm.
- 8+ weeks: add volume from the [case libraries](/case-libraries) (MBA casebooks included here), read one business article daily to build business judgment, and consider 1–2 paid coaching sessions in the final week (PrepLounge handbook of coaches).
