# MBB Case Interview Playbook

Your complete, source-grounded preparation guide for case interviews at **McKinsey, BCG, and Bain**.

This playbook assumes you already understand the basics of what a case interview is and are familiar with the general structure. It goes deep on:

- **How to solve each case type** — the framework, the step-by-step approach, the key factors, the pitfalls, and worked examples
- **How the Big 3 differ** — interview format (interviewer-led vs candidate-led), online assessments (Solve, Casey, SOVA), fit/behavioral portions, culture, and what each firm emphasizes
- **What to practice with** — a ranked list of every serious resource, from free firm materials to courses, drill platforms, and peer marketplaces

## How to use this playbook

1. **Start with the basics** — re-ground yourself on what interviewers actually score ([case-interview-basics](/case-interview-basics)).
2. **Check the roadmap** — a week-by-week plan ([prep-roadmap](/prep-roadmap)).
3. **Master the core 8 case types** — profitability, market entry, M&A, pricing, growth strategy, market sizing, operations/cost, and new product launch. These cover the overwhelming majority of first-round cases.
4. **Skim the extended case types** — valuation, competitive response, marketing, public sector, digital transformation, PE due diligence, revenue growth. Real cases increasingly blend these in.
5. **Read the firm pages** — pick your target firm's page and adjust your practice accordingly.
6. **Use the case libraries** — McKinsey/BCG/Bain official practice cases and the MBA casebook PDFs included in this site.

## The one principle behind everything

Interviewers at MBB do not reward memorized frameworks — they explicitly penalize candidates who recite them. They reward candidates who **build a tailored structure for the case in front of them, drive the analysis with hypotheses, do fast clean math, and synthesize a recommendation**. Every page in this playbook treats frameworks as starting points to adapt, never scripts to recite.

## Key facts to anchor your expectations

- McKinsey, BCG, and Bain each accept roughly **1% of applicants**. Of those who interview, roughly 20–40% pass the first round.
- **Profitability is the most common case type** — roughly 25–40% of first-round cases. Market entry and growth strategy follow. Together, profitability, market entry, and growth account for over half of all cases.
- **McKinsey runs interviewer-led cases** (the interviewer directs the flow); **BCG and Bain run candidate-led cases** (you drive) — though Bain has recently added standardized interviewer-led first round cases in many offices. Practice both styles.
- Every McKinsey interview also contains a **PEI** (Personal Experience Interview) that counts for ~50% of your score. BCG and Bain weave fit questions in more conversationally.
- All three firms now use **online assessments before interviews**: McKinsey Solve, BCG Casey (plus Pymetrics/CCA at some offices), Bain SOVA/TestGorilla/HireVue.
